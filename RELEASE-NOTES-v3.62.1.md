# ProTrackAI v3.62.1: the new ProTrack colours in the emails

v3.61 changed the report documents' emailed header to the new logo's colours but missed three email templates. They still had the old dark header band, with a white "Pro" and a light-blue "Track":
- the daily report approval email;
- the test email (Settings → Email);
- the report emails (released weekly and monthly reports).

They now open with a white header in the new logo's colours: a navy "Pro" (#061945), a blue "Track" (#016AE9) and a blue rule under the header. Emails carry no images, so the name is lettered, as before.
